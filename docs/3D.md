## Architecture
- `src/render/renderer.js` — **façade**. All modules import it. Loads `render3d/fp.js` by default; falls back to
  `render/renderer2d.js` (the old isometric renderer, renamed) on `?view=2.5d` or if WebGL/Three fails.
  Extra 3D-only exports: `is3d()`, `viewMode()`, `turnBy(n)`, `relDir(f, r)`.
- `src/render3d/fp.js` — the 3D view. A WebGL canvas `#world3d` is inserted *under* `#world`; `#world` stays on
  top (transparent) so `input.js` gets all pointer events, and is used as the 2D overlay for effects.
  - World: one `InstancedMesh` per terrain key (16x16 procedural noise texture from `TERRAIN[k].color`; solids get
    brick lines). Seen tiles within `DRAW_R = 22`; not-visible-now tiles tinted `MEMORY`. Rebuilt only when
    turn / hero tile / map / art / camp changes (`builtSig`).
  - Heights: ground `heightAt * ZG(0.11)`, liquids sunk to -0.12, walls `WALL_H 1.9`, rocks/pyramids `heightAt * ZS(0.24)`.
  - Billboards (`THREE.Sprite`, nearest filter, alphaTest) from `art/assets.js` sprites: props (tall 1.35, low 0.6,
    none on the hero's own tile), items, NPCs, monsters (boss 1.6), campfire. `PX = 26` art px per world unit.
  - Camera: eye `EYE 0.62` above ground, eased yaw/pitch, step bob. Yaw 0 looks toward −y (north).
  - Light: `lighting.ambient()` → hemisphere + sun tint, sky/fog colour; torch point light at night and in dungeons.
  - Picking: raycast sprites + instanced meshes (`tiles` Int32Array per mesh). `tileToClient` projects for popups.
  - Effects: `render/fx.js` `drawFx` runs on the overlay with a 3D `fxView` (`toScreen` returns `k` = art px →
    device px at that depth, ×0.6, capped; hero's own tile anchors at lower screen centre). Hit flash = additive
    duplicate billboard. Hurt shake nudges the camera.
- `src/input/input.js` (3D only): W/S ↑/↓ forward/back, A/D ←/→ turn 90° (Shift 45°, free), Q/E diagonal ahead,
  Z/C sidestep, Space wait. Drag = look (`panBy` → yaw/pitch). Tap = inspect, double-tap = auto-walk.
- `src/render/fx.js`: `project()` honours `c.k`; floating text restyled to the title-logo look (both views).

## Glide movement + view pass (user playtest feedback)
Feedback: steppy/jerky; turning stopped you (key repeat); after a diagonal attack you were stuck diagonal (auto-face on
hurt + 45° turn snapping); adjacent creatures towered; wanted longer render distance, slightly zoomed out.
User chose: **Glide** movement, **click-drag** look (no pointer lock).
- `fp.js` `glide()`: held keys (`hold(k)`: f/b/l/r move, tl/tr turn) move a continuous position `px,py` at `SPEED 3.2`
  tiles/s; crossing into a new tile calls `turn.act(dx,dy)` (one turn per tile, diagonals by cutting corners, slides
  along walls). Blocked/attacked → clamp inside own tile; repeat bumps every `SWING 260` ms. Idle → settles to tile centre
  (also follows auto-walk). Turning is continuous (`TURN_RATE 2.4` rad/s); 45° snapping and auto-face-on-hurt removed.
- Camera `BACK 0.4` behind the hero, FOV 80, step bob from distance walked.
- `DRAW_R 32` (CAP 4096), day fog to the draw edge, night fog `radius*2+4`; sprites/items/NPCs/monsters drawn to 30 tiles.
- Smaller billboards: creatures 0.9 (boss 1.25), tall props 1.15, low props 0.5, items 0.65.
- `input.js`: `HOLD3D` map; keydown holds (no throttle), keyup releases, window blur releases all.
  Façade exports `hold`, `releaseAll` (replaced `turnBy`).

## Sky, horizon, planet curve (user: terrain far / monsters 9; sun+moon by time; north star; clouds)
- Planet maths: world is a 768x768 torus (`WS`); drawn as a sphere of the same circumference, R = 768/2π ≈ 122.2 tiles.
  Drop at distance d: `R - sqrt(R² - d²)` (≈0.33 at 9 tiles, ≈1.6 at 20, ≈6.6 at 40). `curve()` applied in `place()` and
  `billboard()`; not in dungeons.
- Overworld terrain drawn to `DRAW_R 44` whether explored or not (unexplored = `MEMORY` haze tint; nothing marked seen,
  minimap unchanged). Monsters/NPCs/items still only within normal sight (isVis). Dungeons: explored-only.
  Tree billboards to 30 tiles, ferns/reeds to 16 (one draw call each). CAP 8192. ~6000 tiles, ~500 sprites.
- Sky (`updateSky`): directions true to the map, north = −y (three −z), east = +x.
  Sun: rises due east at t 0.25, peaks slightly south, sets due west at t 0.78 (title-screen sun: yellow, orange bands).
  Moon: same arc half a day later. North star: fixed due north, bright at night, faint by day.
  Clouds: 18 title-style puffs (white over #9fd8ff), drift west, tinted by the day light; move only when a frame draws.
- Billboards now take the light colour (`lit`) so trees/creatures darken at night (sprites are unlit materials).
- `renderStats().sky` = {t, sun dir, yaw} for tests.
